PLAN AHEAD

Common Application Requirements

Requirements vary by program, but preparing these items early can make the application process easier.

Academic transcripts and a strong academic record
Valid passport or required travel documents
Statement of Purpose or personal statement
Recommendation letters, if required
English proficiency evidence, if required
Proof of enrollment or student status, if required
CV, resume, or record of extracurricular activities
Application forms and supporting documents
