Smart Problem-Solving Tips

01

Understand the problem. Identify the input, output, and constraints.

02

Start with brute force. Find a correct solution before optimizing.

03

Analyze complexity. Consider time and space complexity.

04

Test edge cases. Try empty inputs where allowed, duplicates, and boundary values.

05

Review editorials. Learn a new approach after making a genuine attempt.

06

Track your learning. Revisit difficult problems instead of memorizing code.
