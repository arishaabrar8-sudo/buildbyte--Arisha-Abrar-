💡 Scholarship Application Tips

  • Maintain a strong academic record.
  • Prepare a clear, professional CV or resume.
  • Write a focused Statement of Purpose (SOP).
  • Request recommendation letters well in advance.
  • Check eligibility requirements before applying.
  • Keep track of deadlines and required documents.
  • Highlight relevant projects, leadership, and volunteer work.
  • Use accurate information and submit complete applications.
