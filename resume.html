📌 Essential Resume Sections

Personal Information

Add your name, professional email, phone number, LinkedIn, GitHub, and portfolio links.

Education

Mention your degree, university, GPA if relevant, graduation year, and relevant coursework.

Technical Skills

Include programming languages, frameworks, databases, development tools, and technologies you know.

Projects

Showcase personal and academic projects. Explain what you built, the technologies used, and the results.

Certifications

List relevant courses, certifications, and technical achievements with the issuing organization.

Experience

Include internships, volunteering, leadership roles, and other relevant professional experience.

✅ ATS-Friendly Resume Tips

  • Keep your resume concise, usually one page for students.
  • Use readable fonts such as Arial or Calibri.
  • Use clear headings like Education, Skills, Projects, and Experience.
  • Avoid complicated layouts, excessive graphics, and text boxes.
  • Include relevant keywords from the job description naturally.
  • Describe achievements with specific results when possible.
  • Use consistent dates, formatting, and bullet points.
  • Submit a PDF unless the employer requests another format.

⚠️ Common Resume Mistakes

  • Spelling and grammar errors.
  • Using an unprofessional email address.
  • Including unnecessary personal information.
  • Listing skills you cannot confidently demonstrate.
  • Writing long paragraphs instead of concise bullet points.
  • Adding projects without explaining your contribution.
  • Using the same resume for every role without tailoring it.
  • Including broken links or inaccurate information.
