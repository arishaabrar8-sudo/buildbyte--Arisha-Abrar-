01

Decide what you want to improve or which challenge you need help solving.

02

Prepare Questions

03

04

Apply & Improve

Act on the advice, track your progress, and adjust your goals when needed.

MAKE THE MOST OF GUIDANCE

How to Be a Good Mentee

Mentorship works best when both people communicate and contribute.

Come Prepared

Bring specific questions and explain what you've already tried.

Respect Their Time

Be punctual, communicate clearly, and agree on realistic expectations.

Accept Feedback

Listen carefully and use constructive feedback to improve your work.

Take Initiative

Follow through on agreed actions instead of waiting for reminders.

Show Appreciation

Thank your mentor and share meaningful updates about your progress.

Growth Comes from Action

A mentor can help you see possibilities and avoid common mistakes, but your progress comes from consistent effort, curiosity, and practice.

Learn with purpose. Improve with consistency.
