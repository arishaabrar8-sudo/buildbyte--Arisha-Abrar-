APPLY SMARTER

Job Search Tips That Make a Difference

A focused job search helps you spend more time on relevant opportunities and strong applications.

01

Customize Your Resume

Highlight the skills, projects, and experience most relevant to each job description.

02

Use Relevant Keywords

Include accurate technical skills and role-specific terminology that matches your actual experience.

03

Build Your Portfolio

Showcase personal and academic projects with clear README files, screenshots, and working demos where possible.

04

Network Professionally

Connect with alumni, attend career events, and contact professionals with thoughtful, personalized messages.

05

Set Job Alerts

Use relevant search terms and locations to receive notifications about newly posted opportunities.

06

Track Every Application

Record application dates, deadlines, job links, responses, and follow-up reminders.

Stay Safe While Job Hunting

Be careful with suspicious job offers. Verify the employer and vacancy through official channels. Never pay a recruiter to secure a job, and never share passwords, banking PINs, or verification codes during recruitment.
