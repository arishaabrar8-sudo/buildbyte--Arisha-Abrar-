Make Every Study Session Count

A few simple habits can make group learning more effective.

Set a Schedule

Choose a regular time that works for everyone.

Define Clear Goals

Decide what the group should learn or complete in each session.

Practice Together

Solve problems, review code, and work on small projects.

Review Progress

Discuss what went well and what needs more practice.

Respect Everyone

Welcome questions, respect different opinions, and share speaking time.

Limit Distractions

Keep sessions focused and take short breaks when needed.
