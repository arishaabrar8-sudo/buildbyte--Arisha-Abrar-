COMMUNICATE WITH CONFIDENCE

Common HR Interview Questions

Prepare honest, structured answers using your own experiences, projects, and achievements.

01

Tell me about yourself.

Introduce your education, relevant skills, projects, and career interests in a clear and concise way.

02

Why do you want to join our company?

Connect your interests and skills with the company's work, products, culture, or mission.

03

What are your strengths and weaknesses?

Share relevant strengths and a genuine area you are improving, along with steps you are taking.

04

Describe a challenge you faced.

Explain the situation, your responsibility, the action you took, and the result using the STAR method.

05

Where do you see yourself in five years?

Discuss realistic professional goals and how the role fits your plans for learning and growth.

06

Why should we hire you?

Highlight your relevant abilities, willingness to learn, and the value you can bring to the team.

Use the STAR Method

S — Situation: Describe the context. T — Task: Explain your responsibility. A — Action: Describe what you did. R — Result: Share the outcome and what you learned.
